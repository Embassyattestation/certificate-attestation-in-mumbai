## Step 2: Understand Apostille, MEA Attestation and Embassy Legalisation

Another important consideration is identifying the correct authentication route for the destination country.

India's Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) describes two broad forms of authentication: apostille and normal attestation. The applicable route depends on the destination country's status under the Hague Apostille Convention and the requirements relevant to the document.

### Apostille for eligible documents

An apostille is used for eligible public documents intended for use in countries where the Hague Apostille Convention applies. Depending on the document and the applicable procedure, preliminary authentication by the designated authority may be necessary.

Applicants should verify the destination country's current status and any requirements imposed by the organisation receiving the document.

### Normal attestation and embassy legalisation

For destinations where apostille is not accepted, normal attestation and any required embassy or consular legalisation may apply.

The sequence is not necessarily identical for every document. It can depend on the issuing authority, document category and applicable government procedures.

Do not assume that every document requires embassy attestation or that apostille automatically guarantees acceptance for every overseas application.

For official guidance, consult the [Ministry of External Affairs' attestation and apostille information](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm).

## Step 3: Check Whether Preliminary Verification Is Required

Preliminary authentication is an important part of preparing certain documents for subsequent MEA attestation or apostille.

The MEA's published guidance explains that documents may need authentication by designated authorities associated with the state or union territory from which they were issued. The appropriate route can vary by document category.

For example, educational documents and personal documents may involve different designated authorities. Commercial documents can follow separate requirements.

Before submitting a certificate for attestation in Mumbai, establish:

1. Which authority issued the certificate.
2. Whether preliminary authentication is required.
3. Which authority is competent to perform that authentication.
4. Whether the document is eligible for the intended apostille or normal attestation route.
5. Whether additional embassy or receiving-organisation requirements apply.

This is particularly relevant when the applicant lives in Mumbai but the certificate originates from another state.

Do not assume that every certificate issued outside Maharashtra must follow one identical process. Confirm the requirements for the particular document and current procedure.

## Step 4: Prepare Your Documents Before Submission

Incomplete paperwork can create avoidable complications. A pre-submission review helps identify issues before the document enters the authentication process.

### Check the certificate details

Review the name, certificate number, issuing institution and other identifying information. Ensure that the document is legible and that the details correspond with the supporting records.

If you notice a discrepancy, ask the relevant issuing authority or receiving organisation how it should be resolved before proceeding.

### Confirm the original-document requirement

Find out whether the procedure requires an original certificate or accepts a particular form of certified documentation. Do not assume that a photocopy or notarised copy is an acceptable substitute for an original document.

### Check translations and supporting records

Some receiving organisations may require a translation, identification documents, application forms or other supporting records. Confirm these requirements in advance.

### Keep a submission record

Maintain copies of the documents and supporting paperwork. When handing over originals, obtain an acknowledgement or receipt and confirm how the documents will be returned.

These basic checks are useful whether your certificate was issued in Maharashtra or elsewhere in India.

Apostille and normal attestation are different routes. Confirm the current requirement before submitting documents.

### Treating every certificate as the same document category

Educational certificates, civil records and other documents can have different authentication requirements. Identify the exact document requested.

### Relying on an unverified processing estimate

Timelines depend on the applicable route and the authorities involved. Obtain a case-specific estimate before making time-sensitive plans.

Authentication addresses the relevant signature, seal or official capacity within the applicable process. It does not guarantee that a foreign employer, university or government department will approve an application or accept a document for every purpose.
